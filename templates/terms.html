Service description

TripSwarm is an AI-powered travel planning application created on 10 July 2026. It uses a LangGraph-based multi-agent architecture to assist users with travel research, planning, itinerary generation, and travel-related questions.

The Service may include the following features and AI agents:

  • Flight Research — researches flight options and relevant travel details based on the trip request and selected preferences.
  • Destination Research — researches destinations, places to visit, hotels to stay at, restaurants, and food-related recommendations.
  • Itinerary Planning — creates personalised day-by-day travel itineraries using the trip details, research results, dates, and user-selected travel preferences.
  • Travel Copilot — answers travel questions using retrieval-augmented generation over user-provided documents and relevant web information when current information is required.
  • Document Management — allows users to upload and manage supported travel documents such as PDF, DOCX, and TXT files for use in Travel Copilot responses.
  • Trips and Reports — stores AI-generated trip plans and reports associated with the user's account.
  • Chat History — maintains conversation threads so users can continue travel-planning and Travel Copilot conversations.
  • Saved Preferences — allows users to provide travel and flight preferences that may be used to personalise generated travel plans when selected or applicable.

TripSwarm is a portfolio and showcase project. It is not a commercial travel agency, licensed financial adviser, booking platform, airline, hotel operator, or travel service provider. Information presented by the Service is provided for planning and informational purposes and does not guarantee the availability, price, quality, or suitability of any travel service.
